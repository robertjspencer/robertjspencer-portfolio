Research · First-author publication

Forecasting energy use across buildings

15.9% lower mean absolute error

New buildings often lack enough data to train reliable forecasting models. I investigated how transformer models can learn from other buildings.

Combining multiple source datasets reduced error by an average of 15.9% for 24-hour forecasts, compared with single-source baselines using the vanilla Transformer.

Read publication (opens in a new tab)
Methods and citation

Compared PatchTST, vanilla Transformer and Informer across 16 building datasets from the Building Data Genome Project 2. Evaluated zero-shot and fine-tuning approaches; the 15.9% result uses a multi-source zero-shot setup.

Published in Energy and Buildings (Q1 journal, 2025).
