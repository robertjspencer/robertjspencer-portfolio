Research

Transformer transfer learning for building energy forecasting

My first-author paper in Energy and Buildings (Q1 journal, 2025) compared PatchTST, vanilla Transformer and Informer across 16 building datasets. Combining multiple source datasets in a zero-shot setup reduced mean absolute error by an average of 15.9% for 24-hour forecasts, compared with single-source baselines using the vanilla Transformer.

Industry

AI systems for nutrition formulation and substantiation

At Radix Nutrition, I led the design and deployment of AI systems across R&D and Operations, combining product ownership with hands-on machine learning and software engineering.

  • Built nutrition optimisation systems that reduced formulation iteration time by over 90%, from weeks to days.
  • Co-developed a product range using AI-optimised formulations, reducing formulation costs by over 25%.
  • Led evidence substantiation for over 150 general-level health claims for a single product line using the FSANZ framework.
  • Developed dashboards, user interfaces and automation tools adopted across R&D and Operations.

Formulation modelling integrated major food composition databases, regulatory requirements, and nutrition rating and nutrient profiling systems.
